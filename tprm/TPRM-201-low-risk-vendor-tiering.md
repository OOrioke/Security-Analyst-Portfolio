# TPRM-201: BrightPath Catering: staff cafeteria service

**Track:** Third-Party Risk (TPRM)  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Source:** Vendor intake form  
**Date:** Review opened 2026-10-16

## Scenario
Facilities wants to sign a new food service vendor for the hospital's staff dining room. They submitted a vendor intake form for review.

- **Vendor:** BrightPath Catering (fictional)
- **Service:** Staff cafeteria food service
- **Data shared:** None
- **System access:** None
- **Annual contract:** $85,000

## My decisions
- **How much security review does this vendor need?** A light review: confirm the intake answers, use standard contract terms, and recheck at renewal
- **What drives the low risk rating most?** No patient data, no system access, and little impact if they fail
- **Best next action** Approve with the light review, document the tier and why, and reassess at renewal

## My analysis
Vendor: BrightPath Catering
Tier: Low (no PHI, no system access, low business impact)
Decision: Approved, light review
Conditions: None
Reassess: At contract renewal, or sooner if the service changes
